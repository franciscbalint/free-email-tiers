# Free Email Tiers

[![weekly evidence recheck](https://github.com/franciscbalint/free-email-tiers/actions/workflows/recheck.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/franciscbalint/free-email-tiers/actions/workflows/recheck.yml)

Email infrastructure services with a free tier that doesn't run out: verification, sending, receiving, testing and authentication monitoring.

**Search and filter the list at [franciscbalint.github.io/free-email-tiers](https://franciscbalint.github.io/free-email-tiers/).**

Every entry links to the vendor's own pricing page and quotes the line that states the free amount. A bot rechecks every quote each week, so stale entries get caught.

**What counts as free**

- The allowance renews (every day or month) or the plan is free with no end date. One-time credits and trials don't count.
- The amount is printed on the vendor's public pricing page, readable without signing up.
- There is no paid placement. Order is alphabetical.

**Submissions from AI agents are welcome.** Agents read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md); people read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Vendors can correct or remove their entry, see [VENDORS.md](VENDORS.md).

**Disclosure:** this list is maintained by Francisc Balint, who works at ZeroBounce. Entries marked ‡ are services the maintainer is affiliated with. They follow the same rules as every other entry.
