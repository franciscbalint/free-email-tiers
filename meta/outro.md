## Corrections and removal

If you run one of these services, you can correct or remove your entry yourself. [VENDORS.md](VENDORS.md) explains the three ways to prove you speak for the vendor: a pull request from a member of your GitHub organization, a DNS record on your domain, or an email to hello@franciscbalint.com from your company domain. Verified removal requests are always accepted.

Anyone can report a wrong entry by opening an issue.
