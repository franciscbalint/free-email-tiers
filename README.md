# Free Email Tiers

Email infrastructure services with a free tier that doesn't run out: verification, sending, receiving, testing and authentication monitoring.

Every entry links to the vendor's own pricing page and quotes the line that states the free amount. A bot rechecks every quote each week, so stale entries get caught.

**What counts as free**

- The allowance renews (every day or month) or the plan is free with no end date. One-time credits and trials don't count.
- The amount is printed on the vendor's public pricing page, readable without signing up.
- There is no paid placement. Order is alphabetical.

**Submissions from AI agents are welcome.** Agents read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md); people read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Vendors can correct or remove their entry, see [VENDORS.md](VENDORS.md).

**Disclosure:** this list is maintained by Francisc Balint, who works at ZeroBounce. Entries marked ‡ are services the maintainer is affiliated with. They follow the same rules as every other entry.

## Email verification

- **[Hunter](https://hunter.io)** - Email finder and outreach platform with an email verifier and API. **Free:** 50 credits per month. _Credits are shared with Email Finder and Domain Search; a verification costs 0.5 credit. Credits don't roll over._ <sub>[source](https://hunter.io/pricing), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[mailboxlayer](https://mailboxlayer.com)** - Email validation REST API with syntax, typo, MX, SMTP and disposable checks. **Free:** 100 API requests per month. _Non-commercial use only; no support._ <sub>[source](https://mailboxlayer.com/pricing), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[MailboxValidator](https://www.mailboxvalidator.com)** - Email validation API and bulk list cleaning. **Free:** 300 API queries per month. _API plan only; the bulk upload plan is a one-time trial._ <sub>[source](https://www.mailboxvalidator.com/plans), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[QuickEmailVerification](https://quickemailverification.com)** - Bulk list verification and a real-time email verification API. **Free:** 100 email verifications per day. _Signup asks for a work email._ <sub>[source](https://quickemailverification.com/plans), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[Reoon Email Verifier](https://www.reoon.com/email-verifier/)** - Single and bulk email verification with a real-time API. **Free:** 20 email verifications per day. _No credit card required._ <sub>[source](https://www.reoon.com/email-verifier/), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[UserCheck](https://www.usercheck.com)** - API that flags disposable, relay and free-provider addresses and checks a domain's MX, SPF and DMARC records. **Free:** 1,000 requests per month. _1 request per second. - **[Verimail](https://verimail.io)** - Email verification for uploaded files and through an API. **Free:** 100 verifications per month. _No credit card required._ <sub>[source](https://verimail.io/pricing), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[ZeroBounce ‡](https://www.zerobounce.net)** - Email verification and scoring through an API, bulk list upload or web app. **Free:** 100 email verifications per month. _Requires signing up with a business or premium email domain._ <sub>[source](https://www.zerobounce.net/pricing), checked 2026-11-11</sub>

## Receiving and forwarding

- **[Cloudflare Email Routing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/)** - Forwards mail sent to custom-domain addresses to verified inboxes or to Workers. **Free:** Unlimited inbound emails on the Workers Free plan. _Workers that process email are billed under Workers pricing._ <sub>[source](https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/platform/pricing/), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[CloudMailin](https://www.cloudmailin.com)** - Receives email and delivers it to your app as an HTTP POST. **Free:** 10,000 emails per month. _512 KB per message. Custom domains are paid._ <sub>[source](https://www.cloudmailin.com/plans-and-pricing), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[Forward Email](https://forwardemail.net)** - Open-source email forwarding and hosting for custom domains. **Free:** Unlimited forwarding for unlimited domains. _Forwarding only, no sending from the custom domain._ <sub>[source](https://forwardemail.net/en/private-business-email), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[ImprovMX](https://improvmx.com)** - Email forwarding for custom domains. **Free:** 500 emails forwarded per day. _1 domain, 25 aliases, 7 days of logs. No sending._ <sub>[source](https://improvmx.com/pricing/), checked 2026-11-11</sub>

## Testing and sandboxes

- **[DebugMail](https://debugmail.io/)** - Hosted fake SMTP server that collects test emails per project for a team to review. **Free:** 1 project, 2 team members, unlimited test emails. _10 MB storage per team; 1 forwarded email per day._ <sub>[source](https://debugmail.io/), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[Mail-Tester](https://www.mail-tester.com/)** - Spam score test for a message sent to a generated address, covering SpamAssassin, authentication and blacklists. **Free:** 3 tests per 24 hours. _Free results are public to anyone with the link and kept for 7 days. The API is paid._ <sub>[source](https://www.mail-tester.com/faq), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[Mailinator](https://www.mailinator.com/)** - Public disposable inboxes for QA, with private domains and an API on paid plans. **Free:** Unlimited public inboxes. _Public inboxes are readable by anyone and messages are kept only briefly._ <sub>[source](https://www.mailinator.com/pricing/), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[MailReach Email Spam Test](https://www.mailreach.co/email-spam-test)** - Spam and inbox placement test that reports where a message lands across seed mailboxes. **Free:** 3 tests per 24 hours. <sub>[source](https://www.mailreach.co/email-spam-test), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[Mailsac](https://mailsac.com/)** - Disposable and private test inboxes with a REST API for reading app emails. **Free:** 1 private address and 1,500 API operations per month, plus public inboxes. _Public inboxes are readable by anyone. 50 stored messages._ <sub>[source](https://mailsac.com/pricing), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[MailSlurp](https://www.mailslurp.com/)** - API and SDKs for creating test inboxes and receiving and checking emails in automated tests. **Free:** 250 emails received and 100 inboxes created per month. _Temporary inboxes only; sending stays inside the sandbox. 1 user._ <sub>[source](https://app.mailslurp.com/pricing/), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[Mailtrap Email Sandbox](https://mailtrap.io/)** - Hosted fake SMTP sandbox that captures test emails from development and staging for inspection. **Free:** 50 test emails per month, 1 sandbox, 1 user. _Each sandbox keeps 10 emails._ <sub>[source](https://mailtrap.io/pricing/), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[Parcel](https://parcel.io/)** - Online email code editor with previews, HTML problem checks, accessibility testing and test sends. **Free:** 50 stored emails and 50 snippets. _Screenshots in real email clients and spam scanning are paid._ <sub>[source](https://parcel.io/pricing), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[Postmark Spam Check](https://spamcheck.postmarkapp.com/)** - JSON API and web form that returns a SpamAssassin score for a raw email. **Free:** Free service; no limit stated. <sub>[source](https://spamcheck.postmarkapp.com/), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[Testmail.app](https://testmail.app/)** - Email testing API (GraphQL and JSON) with unlimited addresses for receiving and querying test emails. **Free:** 100 emails per month, unlimited addresses and users. _1 day of retention and one random namespace. New accounts start on a 14-day trial, then move to Free._ <sub>[source](https://testmail.app/pricing/), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[Warmup Inbox Placement Test](https://www.warmupinbox.com/tools/placement-test/)** - Inbox placement test that sends to seed inboxes at Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo and iCloud and checks SPF, DKIM, DMARC and blacklists. **Free:** Free placement tests; no limit stated. _No signup needed._ <sub>[source](https://www.warmupinbox.com/tools/placement-test/), checked 2026-11-11</sub>

## Authentication and deliverability monitoring

- **[Cloudflare DMARC Management](https://developers.cloudflare.com/dmarc-management/)** - Processes DMARC reports for domains on Cloudflare and shows sending sources and SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass rates. **Free:** No limit stated; included on every Cloudflare plan, including Free. _The domain must be a Cloudflare zone. Still labelled beta._ <sub>[source](https://developers.cloudflare.com/dmarc-management/), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[DMARC Report](https://dmarcreport.com/)** - Parses DMARC aggregate reports; operated by DuoCircle. **Free:** 1 domain, 10,000 DMARC messages per month, 30 days of history. _Aggregate reports only. No credit card required._ <sub>[source](https://dmarcreport.com/pricing/), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[HetrixTools Blacklist Monitor](https://hetrixtools.com/)** - Checks IP addresses and domains against DNS blacklists and Microsoft SNDS and alerts on changes. **Free:** 32 blacklist monitors checked every 24 hours, 15 days of history. <sub>[source](https://hetrixtools.com/pricing/blacklist-monitor/), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[Mailhardener](https://www.mailhardener.com/)** - Processes DMARC, TLS-RPT and related email security reports. **Free:** 1 domain, 1 user, up to 1 month of data retention. _For personal or evaluation use._ <sub>[source](https://www.mailhardener.com/pricing), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[MxToolbox](https://mxtoolbox.com/)** - DNS and email diagnostics, with blacklist monitoring in the free account. **Free:** Weekly blacklist monitoring for 1 domain. _Requires a free account._ <sub>[source](https://mxtoolbox.com/c/products/pricing), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[Postmark DMARC Digests](https://dmarc.postmarkapp.com/)** - Processes a domain's DMARC aggregate reports and emails a weekly summary. **Free:** Weekly DMARC digest covering the top 10 sending sources, 7 days of history. _Email reports only, no dashboard. Your DMARC record must send reports to Postmark._ <sub>[source](https://dmarc.postmarkapp.com/), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[Suped](https://www.suped.com/)** - DMARC monitoring and hosted DMARC, with deliverability monitoring and alerts. **Free:** 1 domain, 1,000 emails per month, 14 days of data retention. _No credit card required._ <sub>[source](https://www.suped.com/pricing), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
- **[ZeroBounce Blacklist Monitoring ‡](https://www.zerobounce.net/services/blacklist-monitoring)** - Monitors a domain or IP address against email blacklists and alerts when it is listed. **Free:** 1 blacklist monitor, scanned every 24 hours. _Part of the free ZeroBounce account._ <sub>[source](https://www.zerobounce.net/pricing), checked 2026-11-11</sub>
